# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, and disrespectful conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take appropriate corrective action when necessary.
